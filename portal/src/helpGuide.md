# How to use the signage portal

This portal runs digital signage: TVs in businesses (restaurants, shops, offices) that show pictures, videos, web pages, widgets, specials and text. You manage everything from this website, on a computer or a phone. The TVs run the free **Premier Signage** app and check in with the portal about every 30 seconds, so changes reach a TV within about 30 seconds of clicking Save.

## Who sees what

- **Staff (the signage company's team)** see every client. The main menu has: Dashboard (all clients, offline screens), White Label & Clients, Screen Monitoring, Reports, Billing, Download App, Profile & Admins.
- **Inside a client** (staff open a client from Clients or the Dashboard): Dashboard, Wizard, Content (Media library, Widgets), Channels, Video walls, Playlists, Screens, Screen Monitoring, Reports, Billing, Logins. **← All clients** goes back to the main level.
- **A client's own login** goes straight into that client's account, with the same menu, and can never see other clients. A client admin can add more logins for their business (Logins page). Clients can't upload the player app.
- Every screen, playlist and media file belongs to one client.

## Clients (staff)

- **White Label & Clients → Clients:** add, edit, pause or delete clients. A client can have a screen limit.
- **Pausing** a client logs their users out and blocks their logins. Their TVs keep playing.
- A client can only be deleted after its screens are removed. Deleting a client deletes its playlists, media and logins too.
- **White label:** portal name, logo (PNG, JPG or WebP) and main color, shown on the login page, the menu and the browser tab.

## Quick start: the Wizard

**Wizard** walks through it step by step: add content → make a playlist → connect a TV → design the layers.

## Media library (Content → Media)

- Drag images or videos onto the page, or tap **Choose files**. Formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP pictures; MP4, WebM, MOV videos; up to 2 GB each.
- **Add a web page:** type a web address; it shows full screen on the TV (some websites block being shown inside other apps).
- Search, rename and delete files. Deleting a file removes it from playlists too. This can't be undone.
- Tip: for TVs, landscape 1920×1080 pictures and MP4 (H.264) videos work best. Very large 4K videos may stutter on cheap TV sticks.

## Widgets (Content → Widgets)

Clock (digital or analog, 12/24 hours, date), weather (no account needed), news feed (RSS), QR code, countdown, and message. Each widget has colors to pick. Widgets are added to playlists like pictures.

## Playlists

- **Playlists → New playlist**, then add media from the list on the right (**Add to playlist**).
- Drag to reorder (or Move up / Move down). Set **Seconds** for each picture, web page or widget. Videos play to their end.
- Click **Save**. Every screen using the playlist updates within 30 seconds.

## Screens

### Connect a TV
1. Install the Premier Signage app on the TV (see Download App below).
2. Open the app; it asks for the portal address the first time, then shows a 6-character code.
3. In the portal: **Screens → Add screen**, type the code and a name. Or scan the **QR code** on the TV's screen with a phone camera: it opens the portal with Add screen already filled in (log in if asked; staff first pick which client the TV is for).

- **Set up before the TV is installed:** in Add screen choose **Set up now, connect the TV later**. Prepare the playlist, schedule and design. It shows "No TV yet". When the TV is there, open the screen, click **Connect TV** and type its code.
- **Replace a TV:** on the screen's page click **Replace TV** and type the new TV's code. It takes over everything; the old TV goes back to showing a code.
- **Remove screen:** the TV goes back to showing a pairing code. Its playlist, schedule and layers are deleted.

### A screen's page
- **Settings:** name, location, notes, main playlist (or a Channel), orientation (landscape, portrait rotated left/right, landscape upside down), volume, "Show name on TV". Click **Save settings**.
- **Schedule:** play different playlists at different days and times (for example a breakfast menu 6–11 am on weekdays). With no schedule the screen always plays its main playlist. Click **Save schedule**.
- **Design layers:** opens the designer (see below).
- **Picture quality** (in the device details): what the TV plays at: **4K**, **2K** (1440p) or **Full HD**. The app picks the best the TV and box can handle (the TV panel, the box's video chip and memory) and switches the TV's output to it. Rotated (portrait) screens and video walls play at the box's normal app size (often Full HD). For 4K, upload 3840×2160 pictures and 4K MP4 videos; on a Full HD box they're shown at Full HD automatically.
- **Live view / screenshot:** ask the TV for a screenshot; it appears within about 30 seconds.
- **Remote control** (reaches the TV at its next check-in, within 30 seconds; if offline it runs when the TV reconnects): Reload content, Clear cache & re-download, Restart app, Identify (the screen name flashes on the TV).

### On the TV itself
Press **BACK three times** on the remote (or long-press on a touch screen) for the player menu: change the portal address, turn on kiosk mode (use as home screen), allow auto-start, or exit. The app starts on boot and restarts itself after a crash. It keeps playing downloaded content when the internet is down.

## Channels

One playlist plus one layers design shared by many screens (for example every TV in a chain of stores). Set a screen's playlist to the channel on its page. Each screen's own schedule still applies. Editing the channel's design updates all its screens.

## Video walls

One big picture or video across several TVs hung next to each other (up to 6 × 6).
- **Video walls → New wall:** choose the grid (columns × rows), the playlist, and which screen hangs in each spot.
- **Fill** crops the picture to fill the wall; **Fit** shows the whole picture. **Gap between screens** makes the picture line up across the TV frames.
- Pictures and videos only (web pages and widgets are skipped), no layers; sound only from the top-left screen. All TVs play in step.
- **Wall effects:** an animation that moves across all the wall's screens: Santa & reindeer, plane with banner (your text or logo), fireworks, shooting stars, balloons, snow, confetti, hearts, leaves.
- While in a wall, a screen's own playlist, channel, schedule and layers are not used.

## The designer (Design layers)

A screen's design has four layers, bottom to top:
1. **Video:** the playlist. It can fill the screen or a smaller area (left 70%, right 70%, top 85% for room for a ticker) to leave room for the other layers.
2. **Specials:** offers with title, price, description and an optional photo, taking turns every few seconds, each limited to certain days and times if wanted. Shown as a side panel (left/right) or a card.
3. **Text & pictures:** text and pictures placed anywhere, plus a scrolling ticker at the top or bottom.
4. **Effects:** an animation on top: snow, confetti, sparkles, bubbles, hearts, leaves, stars, Santa & reindeer, fireworks, shooting stars, balloons, or a plane pulling a banner with your text or logo.

How the designer works (like a simple video editor):
- **Toolbar** (top): settings for whatever is selected: font (5 fonts), size, bold, italic, alignment, text color, box color and opacity, width (in % of the screen), snap to a spot, Order (over / under), Duplicate, Delete.
- **Preview** (middle): tap text or a picture to select it, drag to move it. Tap the video area to select the video layer.
- **Side panel tabs:**
  - **Templates:** ready-made designs (menu board, daily deal, price list, coffee shop, happy hour, grand opening, big sale, new arrivals, welcome lobby, holiday, Valentine's Day, fall harvest, Santa's sleigh, plane with banner…). Applying one replaces the design; **Undo** brings the old one back until you save. **💾 Save this design as a template** keeps the current design under **My templates** to reuse on the client's other screens. Staff can tick "Share with all clients" (shared templates leave out the client's pictures). The × on a saved template deletes it; screens using it keep their design.
  - **✨ AI:** describe the screen in your own words ("Taco Tuesday, 3 tacos and a drink for $7.99, festive") and the AI designs it with the client's name and pictures. "Write new words, keep my layout" only rewrites the text (to change an offer or translate). The AI only uses prices you give it. Each client has a daily limit of AI requests.
  - **Media:** tap a picture to place it on the screen. With no picture selected, **Picture effect (all pictures)** sets an effect for every picture. With a picture selected (tap **Unselect** to go back), the next picture you tap is added after it in the same spot and row: a slideshow with crossfades.
  - **Text:** presets (Heading, Subheading, Body text, Price list, Label, Neon sign, Glowing heading) and the **Scrolling ticker** (messages one per line, top or bottom, speed, size, colors).
  - **Specials**, **Effects**, **Video:** the settings of those layers.
- **Moving and resizing on the screen:** drag a text or picture to move it. The selected one gets a purple frame with dots: drag a **corner dot** to make it bigger or smaller (pictures keep their shape; the opposite corner stays put), and on text the **side handles** set how wide it is before it wraps. Works with a finger on a phone too.
- **Over / under (when things overlap):** in the toolbar, **Order**: ⤒ to front (over everything), ↑ forward (over the next thing it overlaps), ↓ backward (under the next thing it overlaps), ⤓ to back. This includes the Specials panel: a picture or text can sit over it or under it. The ticker and effects always stay on top. To pick something that's underneath another item, **click it again**: each click selects the next one down at that spot.
- **Text and picture effects** (when a text or picture is selected): Glow, Neon glow, Shadow, Shine (a light sweeps across), Grow (gentle pulse), each with its own color.
- **Entrance and exit transitions** for each text or picture: fade, small → big, big → small, slide in from the left/right/top/bottom, spin, pop; transition length 0.2 to 5 seconds. **▶ Preview** plays that part.
- **Timeline** (bottom): one row per layer with an on/off box. Text and picture bars can be dragged to set when they show, and their ends dragged to make them longer or shorter. ▶ plays; tap the ruler to jump. **Timeline length** is how often timed text and pictures repeat on the TV.
  - Texts and pictures can share a timeline row and take turns: drag a bar up or down onto another row, or onto "+ New row". Where two bars on a row overlap, the overlap becomes the transition (a crossfade unless another transition is picked).
  - **Select several:** hold Ctrl or Shift and click items on the screen or bars on the timeline (on a phone tap **☐ Select several**, then tap them). Then drag one bar to move them all, drag an end to stretch them all, use **Same start / Same end** to line them up. **Clear selection** or Esc unselects.
  - **Groups:** with several selected, tap **🔒 Group** (on the purple frame around them) or **🔒 Lock as group** (toolbar). A group stays together: tapping any member selects all of it; on the screen, drag to move them together and drag a **corner dot** to make the whole group bigger or smaller (everything keeps its place in the group); on the timeline its bars move and stretch together (locked bars show 🔒). **🔓 Ungroup** on the frame (or Unlock group in the toolbar) takes it apart.
- The Delete key removes the selected items. Nothing reaches the TV until you click **Save changes**. Leaving the page without saving asks first.

## Screen Monitoring

Which screens are online now, uptime per hour (last 24 hours and 7 days), a wall of the latest screenshots, and "Screenshot all". A screen is offline when it hasn't checked in for a few minutes (no power, no internet, app closed).

## Reports (proof of play)

The TVs report every item they played. See plays and time per day, per content and per screen, and export a CSV file. Kept 13 months.

## Billing

Shows each client's plan, monthly amount and paid / overdue status from the billing app (read only). Staff link each client on the Billing page.

## Download App (installing the player)

- **Android TV, Google TV, Android boxes and tablets (Android 5.0 or newer):** one app works on all of them. Download the APK from the Download App page (or type the portal address followed by /download/PremierSignage.apk in the TV's browser or the Downloader app) and install it. Allow "install unknown apps" when asked.
- **Amazon Fire TV Stick:** install the free **Downloader** app from the Amazon app store; Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps → turn on Downloader (no Developer options? Settings → My Fire TV → About → click the device name 7 times); in Downloader type the portal address followed by /download/PremierSignage.apk and install.
- **Updates** reach the TVs on their own; on most boxes someone presses **Install** with the remote once per update.
- Staff see **App problems reported by TVs** on this page when a player app crashes.
- Not supported: Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) smart TVs' own systems, Roku, Apple TV. Use an Android TV box or Fire TV Stick plugged into those TVs.

## Profile & Admins / Logins

Change your password and manage logins. Staff add team logins; a client admin adds logins for their business.

## Help button

The **? Help** button (bottom right of every page) answers questions about the portal. Tap **📎** to add a screenshot or a photo (for example a photo of the TV or a screenshot of an error); on a computer you can also paste a screenshot. On a screen's page (or its designer), tick **Show the AI this TV's latest screenshot** so it can see what that TV is showing; use **📷 Take screenshot** first for a fresh one. Each login has a daily number of questions.

## Common problems

- **The TV doesn't show my changes:** did you click Save? Wait 30 seconds. Check the screen is online (Screen Monitoring). Use Remote control → Reload content, or Clear cache & re-download.
- **Screen shows offline:** check the TV has power and internet, and that the Premier Signage app is open. Turn on auto-start and kiosk mode in the player menu (BACK three times).
- **The TV shows a code again:** the screen was removed or replaced in the portal; add it again with Screens → Add screen.
- **A video doesn't play or stutters:** use MP4 (H.264), 1080p. Very big files take time to download first.
- **A web page is blank:** some websites refuse to be shown inside other apps; use a screenshot picture or a widget instead.
- **Text looks blurry or blocky on a big 4K TV:** many TV sticks draw their apps at 1080p or 720p. Videos and pictures play at 4K when the box can (see Picture quality), but text and effects on top are drawn at the box's app size. Keep effects like neon on medium-size text, and use the newest player app.
- **The app keeps closing on a TV:** staff can see the error under Download App → App problems reported by TVs; send it to whoever maintains the app.
